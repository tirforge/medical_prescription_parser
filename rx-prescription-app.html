How verification works

Three checks behind every result.

Every line gets proven

Exact, auto-corrected, or salt-level match against the offline registry — with maker, pack, and a confidence score. Unknown spellings stay flagged, never silently fixed.

Plain words first

Side effects, dosage timing, and warnings rewritten for patients. Full tables, labels, and JSON stay one disclosure away for doctors.

Careful by default

Blurry photos get flagged before upload credit is spent. Clearing history asks first. The final proof is always the QR on your own pack.
